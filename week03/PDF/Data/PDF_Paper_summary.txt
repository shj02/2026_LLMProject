# A Design and Implementation of Online Exhibition Application for Disabled Artists

## Author's problem identification and argument

The authors identify a significant issue faced by disabled artists: the lack of exhibition spaces and opportunities to share their work with the public. Despite growing interest in art created by disabled individuals, there are limited venues for showcasing and promoting their work, which hinders their ability to reach wider audiences and gain recognition. The authors argue that an online exhibition platform can address these challenges by providing a virtual space where disabled artists can display their work without the constraints of physical exhibition spaces. The proposed Android-based application incorporates features like Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) to enhance accessibility for visually and hearing-impaired users. It also includes a disability certification process to ensure that only authenticated disabled artists can exhibit their work. By utilizing technologies such as MySQL for database management and Firebase Storage for handling large data sizes, the application aims to overcome data limitations and facilitate better communication between disabled artists and the general public. The authors emphasize the need for a simple and cost-effective registration process, considering the physical and economic challenges faced by disabled artists. They propose a design that accommodates various types of disabilities and artistic styles, aiming to create an inclusive environment that supports the artistic creativity of disabled individuals.

## Introduction Author

Seung Gyeom Kim, Ha Ram Kang, Tae Hun Kim, Jun Hyeok Lee, and Won Joo Lee are affiliated with Hanyang University and Inha Technical College in Korea. They have backgrounds in applied artificial intelligence, computer science, and engineering, with research interests spanning computer vision, human-computer interaction, mobile programming, web programming, and machine learning. Their collective expertise informs the development of the online exhibition application, aiming to leverage technology to enhance accessibility and opportunities for disabled artists.
